# T2 Redesign: Android beta

The beta of the new Trenches II, for testers on the T2 Discord.

## Download

**[Download the newest beta (APK)](https://github.com/rowanmorse-demo/T2-Redesign-Beta/releases/latest/download/T2-Redesign.apk)**

This link always gets the newest beta. Every earlier beta is listed under [Releases](https://github.com/rowanmorse-demo/T2-Redesign-Beta/releases).

## Installing

1. Open the download link on your Android phone (Android 10 or newer).
2. Open the downloaded `T2-Redesign.apk`. If Android asks, let your browser install apps (Settings > Install unknown apps).
3. If Play Protect warns about an unknown app, tap **More details**, then **Install anyway**. The beta isn't from the Play Store, so Play Protect doesn't know it yet.
4. It installs as **T2 Redesign**, next to Trenches II. Your normal game isn't changed.

To update, download from the same link and install it over the old one.

## What to expect

- You play other beta testers, on Android and on iPhone (TestFlight), and never people on the normal game.
- The beta starts you as a new player. Your progress in the normal game stays in the normal game.
- Real-money purchases don't work in the beta.
- Report bugs and ideas in the T2 Discord. A screenshot helps.
